FAQ

Before you download.

Does Fieldsmith upload my documents?

No. Analysis, OCR and exports run on your own Windows PC. The internet is used only to activate and check the license.

Where do the forms work?

Fieldsmith writes standard fillable PDF forms (AcroForm) that open in Adobe Acrobat Reader, Chrome and Edge. Word forms open in Microsoft Word.

Can it make a form from a scan or a photo?

Yes. Fieldsmith straightens the page, cleans the paper, reads the text (OCR) and then finds the fields. OCR languages included: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Latvian, Russian and Ukrainian.

What does the free trial include?

Every tool, for 7 days or 10 launches. Saved files may cover up to 5 pages per launch. Enter a license key at any time to remove the limits.

On how many computers can I use one license?

Up to 2 computers at the same time. Moving to a new PC? Use Help → License → Deactivate This Computer on the old one first.

Is this a subscription?

No. Fieldsmith is a one-time purchase for personal, professional and commercial use.

Can I get a refund?

Please try the free trial first. If the full version does not work on your computer because of a defect that support cannot solve, contact us within 14 days of the purchase for a refund.

Does it run on a Mac?

No. Fieldsmith is a Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) application.
